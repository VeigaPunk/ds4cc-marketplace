Shared policy

  • CDP on 127.0.0.1 only. Non-loopback rejected unless an explicit dangerous override.
  • Never attach to the daily browser. Isolated --user-data-dir is mandatory.
  • No cookie export, no session JSON, no passwords in the repo.
  • Sequential fires only. Wait for confirmation before the next CLI.
  • Official binaries keep their names: grok and kimi stay vendor CLIs.
  • Pin a dedicated tab per site if you also chat there by hand.
