SECOND WIND ARCADE — CREDITS

Original art, characters, layouts, procedural animation, sound design and music
were authored for this collection. The illustrated scenes, authored level data
and tested rule engines preserve work from the supplied checkpoint.

Crown & Sand — procedural illustrated arena and lute/drum score
Deadlock Rooms — original block silhouettes, industrial rooms and tension score
Starfall Flock — authored SVG star-creatures and eleven space sectors
Cluck Horizon — authored courier, flock, bosses and original campaign
Vector Vault — original chamber atlas, geometry language and electronic cues
Pulsebound — five original charts and synchronized musical arrangements
Burger Tycoon — flat operational scenes and original corporate-music sequence

PixiJS renders Deadlock Rooms and the two shared-engine shooter campaigns.
PixiJS is licensed under the MIT License, included at licenses/pixi-js.txt.
All fonts use the browser's local system fonts. No assets are fetched from
outside this release at runtime.

Verification tools and their separate notices are outside the playable site,
in the submission records. They are not required to play.
