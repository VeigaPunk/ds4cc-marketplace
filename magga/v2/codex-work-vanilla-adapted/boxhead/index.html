READ THE ROOM

Green walkers swarm. Red runners flank. Armored brutes absorb fire. Purple wardens flash before firing energy bolts. Orange volatiles explode at close range. Break barrels from a distance; blast chains hurt everyone nearby.

Co-op: a cleared wave revives fallen partners. Deathmatch: every weapon is available from the start. First to five wins; defeated players respawn with brief protection.
