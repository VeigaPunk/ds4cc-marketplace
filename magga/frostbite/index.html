THE IGLOO

Every white floe you land on turns blue and adds a block; when all four rows are blue they turn white again. Sixteen jumps raise the igloo and its door — then get back to shore, stand under it and jump in before the temperature falls from 45° to zero.

THE HAZARDS

Snow geese, king crabs and killer clams shove you into the sea; fish are 200 points. From level 4 a polar grizzly prowls the shore — lure him off, he cannot reach the far-left corner. Four igloos a shift: day, then night. A bonus builder every 5,000 points.
